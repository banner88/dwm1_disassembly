# Play here

**▶ Play here** (Rooms tab, on the screen / state row — or **F5**) starts the game in
the room you are looking at, from the selected cell (or the walkable cell nearest
the middle of the screen), with a game state of your choice. It works for rooms in any region
(S140) — it lands in the room of THIS region, even where another region has a room with
the same number (S141, checked in the emulator). When the project
changed since the last build it is saved and built first (a few seconds). The game
runs in the Playback window: arrows, Z / Space = A, X = B, Enter = Start;
**⟲ Restart** starts it again with the same state.

The first press asks for the game state; afterwards ▶ Play here plays at once with
the state you chose last (per project). Change it with the ▾ next to the button →
**Game state…**:

- **A new game** — the hero, no monsters, no story flags.
- **My save file** — CONTINUE from a battery save (.sav) of THIS project's build: its
  flags, party and farm, then straight into this room. A save made in a room this build
  no longer has (deleted, or from another build) first goes home, as in the game — Play
  here waits for that, then takes you into this room (S141; before, it stayed in the
  Castle).
- **A story point of the original game** — a slider over the game's order (the 31
  gates, the arena classes G to S, Starry Night, Monster Grandpa — the same steps as
  the Balance tab): "about to do the Gate of Peace". The story flags and room states
  are what the game's own scripts write for every step done before it (the bosses'
  win scenes, the King's speeches, the arena victories, the ending — the same as the
  real game, checked step by step in the emulator). The party is the team the
  Balance tab expects there: a player's kit at the level the step's hardest fight
  needs (or a strong / casual roll; **Reroll** for another; untick *level: what this
  point needs* to set the level yourself).
- **A story point of my project** — the same slider for your project: your gates at
  the game's places (as on the Balance tab), each new gate or world right after the
  gate it was copied from; your re-bossed / new gates done before the point count as
  cleared (their own cleared flags), and the team uses your project's data.
- **Set by hand** — up to three monsters: species, level, plus, and the skills as the
  game raises them or picked (up to 8). A monster cannot start below the level its
  enemy row joins at.

For every choice you can also turn flags **ON** or **OFF** (your named flags, the
gates' cleared flags, the arena classes, any number) — to try a room's other states.
With the Milly hook on you play as Milly (and her arrival scene counts as seen).

Limits: the party replaces party slots 1-3 (a save's farm is kept); items and gold
are the new game's; the vanilla story state does not include side quests the game
leaves to the player (the farm, the library, medals) — turn their flags on if a room
tests them.
