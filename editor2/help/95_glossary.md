# Glossary

Every word the editor uses, A to Z, in one or two sentences — each with a link to
the topic that explains it. (S167. Search the help for a word, or press **F1** /
the round **?** button anywhere in the editor to open the help of what you are
looking at.)

## A

**Animated tiles** — tiles of a room that move (water, torches): borrowed from a
game room, or your own frames drawn in the editor. [Animated tiles](15_animated_tiles.md)

**Animation (battle)** — what a skill shows in battle; new ones are made from the
frames of the game's 45. [Animations](57_animations.md)

**Arena** — the game's class matches, Starry Night and Monster Grandpa's match;
**Your arena** = your own copies of the arena rooms. [Arena](53_arena.md),
[Your arena](69_your_arena.md)

**Archive** — a breeding-hint object that lists one family's crosses the player
has learned. [Breeding hints](78_hints.md)

**Area** — a folder for your rooms in the editor's lists; the game never sees it.
[Rooms](10_rooms.md)

## B

**Balance** — how hard each key fight is, as the team level a player needs to win.
[Balance](72_balance.md)

**Base (skill)** — a NEW skill works like the game skill it is based on: the same
effect, and enemies use it as they use the base. [Skills](54_skills.md#New%20skills)

**Battle list / encounter list** — the wild monsters (and their chances) a gate
floor or a room fights. [Encounters](59_encounters.md)

**Borrow** — take tiles or colours from another room (or a gate theme) into yours.
[Tilesets, metatiles and palettes](13_tilesets.md), [Rooms](10_rooms.md)

**Boss floor** — the last floor of a gate, with its boss; yours can be a room of
your own. [Boss floors](70_boss_floors.md)

**Breeding pool** — the list a breeder NPC rolls their monster from, by the
player's progress. [Breeding NPCs](68_breeding_npcs.md#Breeding%20pools)

**Build** — turn the project into a ROM (Ctrl+B); the original ROM is never
changed. [Build](80_build.md)

## C

**Capacity** — every limit the project can reach and how much of it is used.
[Capacity](81_capacity.md)

**Cast member** — a person or monster that appears only in a cutscene.
[Cutscenes](63_cutscenes.md)

**Conversation** — what an NPC says and does when talked to: texts, YES / NO,
flags, items, battles, moves. [Conversations](40_conversations.md)

**Copy (room)** — a new room made from one of yours, with its own scripts, texts
and (unconnected) doors. [Rooms](10_rooms.md)

**Cutscene** — a scene that plays in a room (on entering, talking, a flag…).
[Cutscenes](63_cutscenes.md)

## D

**Depth** — how many breeding steps a monster is from monsters you can get without
breeding (0 = no breeding needed). [Breeding](58_breeding.md#By%20monster)

**Door** — a two-way link between two rooms (placed on art that looks like a
door). [Doors, teleports and the World tab](11_doors.md)

## E

**Earth** — the 27th resistance this editor adds (Quake and your new skills can
test it). [Resistances](79_resistances.md)

**Enemy row** — one monster as it is fought: species, level, stats, AI, whether it
can join. [Enemies](50_enemies.md), [Monsters](52_monsters.md)

**Examine spot** — a cell the player presses A while facing (a sign, a mural).
[NPCs, monsters, spots and doors](20_npcs.md)

## F

**Family** — Slime, Dragon … ???; Spirit is the 11th. It decides the library tab,
family breeding and the arena voice. [Families](51_families.md)

**Family recipe** — the default breeding rule: family × family → a monster of the
first parent's family. [Breeding](58_breeding.md#Family%20recipes)

**Flag** — an ON / OFF switch saved with the game; your flags have names.
[Flags](30_flags.md)

**Flag variant** — another encounter list used while some flags hold.
[Encounters](59_encounters.md)

## G

**Gate** — a portal dungeon of random maze floors; yours too (a copy of a game
gate). [Gates](60_gates.md)

**Gate theme** — one of the 16 maze looks of the gates, usable in your rooms.
[Gate themes](12_gate_themes.md)

## H

**Hint** — a breeding cross a monument teaches; the player learns it by reading it
(or breeding it). [Breeding hints](78_hints.md)

**Hub** — where the game sends the player "home" (a lost battle, the WarpWing…).
[The hub](66_hub.md)

## I

**Item** — the game's 43 items and your own (44-127), each working like one of
the game's. [Items](74_items.md)

## J

**Join version** — the monster the player gets instead of the one they fought
(the game does this for its story bosses: a strong fight row, a weaker one that
joins). [Enemies](50_enemies.md)

## L

**Line set** — the words a service NPC (Vault, farm…) says in its menus; one set
can serve many NPCs. [Services](67_services.md)

**Locked exit** — a door shut until a check holds. [Doors](11_doors.md#Locked%20doors)

## M

**Make editable** — copy one of the game's rooms into your project so you can
change it. [Rooms](10_rooms.md)

**Memorial / statue** — a statue that shows a monster that won a big battle with
the player and has left since. [The memorial](77_memorial.md)

**Metatile** — the painting unit of a room: 2 × 2 tiles with their palette
slot (one for all four, or one per tile).
[Tilesets, metatiles and palettes](13_tilesets.md#The%20metatile%20editor)

**Milly hook** — the game goes on as Milly after the dresser scene: her sprite,
name and arrival room. [The Milly hook](64_milly_hook.md)

**Monument** — an examine spot that teaches the player one breeding cross.
[Breeding hints](78_hints.md)

## N

**New game** — where a new game starts and what counts as already played.
[Your own opening](75_opening.md)

**NPC** — a person or monster in a room; what it says is its talk or conversation.
[NPCs, monsters, spots and doors](20_npcs.md)

## O

**One-only item** — an item that is used up 0 %: the player can hold one at most.
[Items](74_items.md)

## P

**Page 2** — the second 128 tiles of a tileset (GBC only); used by itself when page 1
is full. [Tilesets, metatiles and palettes](13_tilesets.md#Page%202%20%E2%80%94%20the%20second%20128%20tiles)

**Palette** — the colours of a room (BG palettes) — your own or borrowed.
[Tilesets, metatiles and palettes](13_tilesets.md#BG%20palettes%20(the%20Palettes%20page))

**Play here** — build and start the game in the room shown, with a game state you
pick. [Play here](16_play_here.md)

**Plus** — the "+" of a bred monster: the higher parent's plus + 1, more with high
levels. [Breeding](58_breeding.md#How%20the%20game%20decides%20an%20egg)

**Portal** — a gate's or a world's entrance in a room. [Gates](60_gates.md),
[Worlds](65_worlds.md)

**Preference (AI)** — how much more or less often enemies use a new skill than its
base (-60 … +60). [Skills](54_skills.md#Monster%20AI)

**Project** — a folder with `project.json` and its assets; everything you make is
in it. [Getting started](00_start.md)

## Q

**Quest** — a task an NPC gives, tracked with the story tools.
[Story checks, quests and the story spine](71_story_quests.md#Quests)

## R

**Region** — the editor's own group of 128 rooms; it hands them out by itself and
never shows them. [Limits](90_limits.md)

**Resistance** — how strongly a monster blocks one kind of attack (27 kinds).
[Resistances](79_resistances.md)

## S

**Screen** — one 10 × 8-cell part of a room; a room is up to 4 × 4 screens.
[Rooms](10_rooms.md)

**Service NPC** — a Vault keeper, farm keeper, librarian, namer, Medal Man, egg
appraiser or gate guide of your own. [Services](67_services.md)

**Shop** — what a shopkeeper sells (up to 20 items) and the prices. [Shops](62_shops.md)

**Special recipe** — a breeding rule for particular monsters, checked before the
family recipes. [Breeding](58_breeding.md#Special%20recipes)

**Special room** — a fixed room a gate serves on some floors instead of a maze
(yours can be one). [Gates](60_gates.md)

**Spirit** — the 11th family this editor adds. [Families](51_families.md)

**State (room)** — another version of a room's screen, shown by flag rules.
[Rooms](10_rooms.md)

**Step-on trigger** — a cell that runs something when the player steps on it.
[Doors](11_doors.md#One-way%20teleports,%20step-on%20triggers,%20stairs)

**Story check** — a question the game answers (items, gold, monsters, levels…),
usable wherever a flag is checked. [Story checks](71_story_quests.md#Story%20checks%20%E2%80%94%20questions%20the%20game%20answers)

**Story point** — a moment of the ORIGINAL story a scene can jump the game's own
rooms to. [Your title screen and your ending](76_ending.md)

**Story spine** — your chapters, in order. [Story checks, quests and the story spine](71_story_quests.md#The%20story%20spine)

**Swirl** — a portal's spinning picture; it stops (or changes colour) once its gate
is cleared. [Gates](60_gates.md)

## T

**Teleport (one-way)** — an exit that sends the player somewhere without a way
back. [Doors](11_doors.md#One-way%20teleports,%20step-on%20triggers,%20stairs)

**Text** — every text of the game, editable on the Text tab; any length.
[Text](56_text.md)

**Tileset** — the sheet of small tiles a room is painted from: 256 in two pages of 128.
[Tilesets, metatiles and palettes](13_tilesets.md)

**Trigger** — what starts a cutscene: entering the room, talking to someone, a
flag. [Cutscenes](63_cutscenes.md)

## V

**Voice** — the blip a text box makes (low, high or none). [NPCs](20_npcs.md#Text)

## W

**Walkability** — whether the player can walk on a cell (the corner dot on a
metatile: red wall, green walkable). [Rooms](10_rooms.md)

**Window words** — the short words in the game's windows (INFO, ITEM, YES / NO…).
[Text](56_text.md#Window%20words%20(S165,%20S166))

**World** — hand-made places of your rooms, entered through a portal like a gate.
[Worlds](65_worlds.md)

**World tab** — the map of your rooms and the doors between them.
[Doors, teleports and the World tab](11_doors.md#The%20World%20tab)
